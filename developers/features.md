
## Social

Emissary natively connects to the open social web, sending and receiving posts, status updates, images, videos, and more from a variety of established social networks.

* Participate in discussions on other **Fediverse** apps like Mastodon, PixelFed, and others via ActivityPub
* Subscribe to **RSS** feeds and realtime **WebSub** from millions of popular websites
* Publish your own posts, images, videos, and more on all these same networks, as well.
* And, Emissary works great with other **IndieWeb** web blogs.
