
Free Registration is a simple registration app that displays a simple signup form on your website.  Anyone who fills out this form gets a free account.

## Custom Content
You can customize a few options on this form, including the header, intro text, confirmation text, and some terms of service for users to acknowledge.

## Custom Rules
You can also specify which Inbox Template and Outbox Template to give users, along with adding them to a Group upon signup.

## Registration Secret
The Free Registration Form requires that you enter a "secret" that is used to authenticate the signup.  This should be a long, random, unguessable value, that helps prevent malicious bots from spamming your signup form.
