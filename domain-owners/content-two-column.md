The Two-Column layout combines two [markdown](/content-markdown) editors into a single page.  Enter your content in either the left or right column and it will be laid out on the page correctly.

## Choosing Column Widths

There are several markers in the top gutter of the page that let you choose the relative widths for each column: 1/4, 1/3, 1/2, 2/3, 3/4. Clicking any of these markers will let you preview the relative widths before saving.

## Responsive Design

On smaller devices, the two columns are collapsed into a single column, with the LEFT column on top.  This is handled for you automatically.
