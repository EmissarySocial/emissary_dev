
Address search is used to provide "autocomplete" hints to users when they enter an address on your Emissary website.  This makes it easy for people to find and enter address locations into your website.

To set up an address search provider, sign in to your Emissary server as a domain owner, navigate to  **Server Settings \> External \> API Keys**.

You'll need to sign up for an account with one of the third-party providers listed below. All include a free tier that will work for small websites without any additional fees.


## Recommended Providers
These providers are recommended because they support a wide range of geographic services, so you can get your website quickly

**[HERE Technologies](https://here.com)** <br>
Here supports a wide range of services, and their autocomplete search is the best we've reviewed.

**[Geoapify.com](https://geoapify.com)** <br>
Geoapify supports a wide range of services, including autocomplete.
