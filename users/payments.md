
Emissary is built to put you, the individual, in control of your online presence.  This extends to payments, allowing you to charge your visitors for access to the things you post online.

## How It Works

Emissary is built to connect to a number of online payment platforms, giving you the freedom to choose what works best for you, and the power to change processors whenever you want.  This prevents you from being "locked in" to any one platform.

We have rolled out support for our first payment processor: [Stripe](https://stripe.com), with plans to add more platforms in the future.

### Link Your Merchant Account
You can get started by going to your **Profile \> Settings \> Payments**.  Here, you can set up a new merchant account or link to an account you already have with a supported payment provider.  *IMPORTANT:* This account will always belong to *you* and not the Emissary server that you link it to.

Linking your merchant account will send secret API keys to Emissary, so that it can initiate transactions on your behalf.  You can revoke these API keys at any time, and sever the connection between Emissary and your merchant account.

Once you've linked your 3rd-party merchant account, you can set up products and subscriptions in that 3rd-party service.  Emissary will list out these each of these products in your **Profile \> Settings \> Payments** page.

## Selling Products with One-Time Payments

Certain Emissary apps allow you to sell access to individual posts.  For example, Bandwagon allows you to sell an individual album.  When a guest purchases the album, they get access to exclusive downloads that the musician has provided.

After you have linked your merchant account, you can set up an individual product for sale by navigating to that product in your user profile, then clicking the "Sell Online" button.  You'll get a step-by-step guide that walks you through the process.  Once you link this item to a product listing your 3rd-party merchant account, visitors will be able to purchase it on your profile.

*NOTE:* It's possible to link an item in your profile to more than one product in your 3rd-party merchant account.  In this case, visitors will get a pop-up with a list of ways to purchase the item.  You might do this, for instance, if you sell albums one at a time, vs. a group price for purchasing all albums at once.

## Using Circles for Private News Feeds

You can also charge for membership in a circle, which grants members access to a private news feed directly from you.  The process is similar to selling individual items.  You'll go to your **Profile \> Settings \> Circles** page then select the product(s) from your 3rd-party merchant account that grant people membership in that Circle.

Every time you make a post, you can choose who gets to see it.  You can make posts public for everyone to see regardless of payment, or you can limit viewership to one or more circles.  If the post is limited, then only members of that circle will be able to see the post.

*NOTE:* It's possible to link a circle to more than one product in your 3rd-party merchant account.  In this case, visitors will get a pop-up with a list of ways to purchase the circle.  You might do this, for instance, if you have different rates for weekly, monthly, or yearly subscriptions.
